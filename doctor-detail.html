Dr. Wasim Abed Aumi

MBBS, FCPS (Pediatrics)

Dr. Wasim Abed Aumi is a highly experienced and dedicated Pediatrician with over 15 years of clinical excellence in newborn care, child development, and pediatric medicine. As the Chairman of Jahanara Memorial Shishu Hospital, he leads the institution with a commitment to providing premium, accessible, and compassionate healthcare to children and infants.

He specializes in treating acute and chronic childhood illnesses, developmental assessments, newborn resuscitation, asthma management, nutrition coaching, and preventive child healthcare. He is renowned for his warm, child-friendly approach that helps kids feel safe and comfortable during medical visits.
