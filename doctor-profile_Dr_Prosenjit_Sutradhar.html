Dr. Prosenjit Sutradhar

MBBS, PGPN (Germany)

Dr. Prosenjit Sutradhar is a skilled Pediatric Specialist with advanced training in Pediatric Nutrition (PGPN) from Germany. He brings years of experience in managing childhood growth disorders, pediatric diet planning, and childhood nutritional deficiencies, ensuring children achieve their healthy developmental milestones.

In addition to general pediatric medicine, his clinical focus includes nutritional counseling, managing digestive disorders, and preventive immunizations. His patient-centric philosophy emphasizes educating parents on optimal nutrition and healthy lifestyle choices for their children.
